> [!socratic]- Socratic session — 2026-11-11
> **Clarification:** Give one concrete example of this idea at work. What does it look like?
> Answer: Contoh: aplikasi mencatat satu kebiasaan harian dengan satu ketukan.
>
> **Assumptions:** What are you taking for granted for this idea to succeed?
> Answer: Orang mau membuka aplikasi tiap hari tanpa pengingat.
>
> **Evidence:** How do you know the problem this idea solves really exists?
> Answer: Pengalaman pribadi: kebiasaan gagal karena terlalu rumit dicatat.
>
> **Viewpoints:** How would someone who disagrees see this idea? What are their reasons?
> Answer: Pengguna pemula senang, pakar mungkin merasa terlalu sederhana.
>
> **Implications:** What must you give up or stop doing so this idea can move forward?
> Answer: Jika berhasil, orang konsisten mencatat; yang tidak terduga: ketergantungan.
>
> **Question about the question:** What do you still not know about this idea, and how could you find out?
